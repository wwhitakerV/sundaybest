SundayBest Paint Intro

The red screen is blank. One quick stroke of white paint goes across it over the letters. Then the whole stroke turns liquid at once and drops under gravity, unevenly, stretching as it falls. It leaves the letters wet, and the paint that fell is the pool. Then it plays as v1: the letters fall in, the pool erupts to full white and holds, and the page arrives under it.
